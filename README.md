## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive Power BI financial analysis dashboard** developed to evaluate the **Q2 financial performance of JPMorgan Chase & Co.**

The dashboard transforms quarterly financial-report data into an interactive and structured analytical view, allowing users to explore:

- Revenue and net income performance
- Business-segment contribution
- Product-level revenue performance
- Quarterly financial trends
- Segment-level profitability
- Key movements in financial performance

The objective is not simply to present financial figures, but to understand **where revenue is generated, which business segments are driving performance, and how financial performance changes across reporting periods.**

---

## 🏦 Company & Business Context

**JPMorgan Chase & Co.** is a global financial services firm operating across consumer banking, commercial banking, investment banking, payments, markets, and asset & wealth management.

For this analysis, the dashboard focuses on the company's major reporting segments:

- **Consumer & Community Banking (CCB)**
- **Commercial & Investment Bank (CIB)**
- **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM)**
- **Corporate**

The analysis particularly focuses on understanding the contribution of these businesses to overall revenue and net income.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 💰 Revenue Analysis

Analyze total revenue and understand how revenue changes across reporting periods.

Key questions addressed:

- How has revenue changed over time?
- Which business segments contribute the most revenue?
- Which product lines generate the highest revenue?
- What are the major quarter-over-quarter movements?

### 📈 Net Income Analysis

Evaluate profitability alongside revenue performance.

The dashboard analyzes:

- Net income trends
- Profitability changes
- Revenue vs. net income movements
- Segment contribution to overall earnings

### 🏦 Business Segment Analysis

The dashboard evaluates performance across JPMorgan Chase's major business segments:

| Segment | Focus |
|---|---|
| Consumer & Community Banking | Consumer banking, cards, home lending and related services |
| Commercial & Investment Bank | Commercial banking, investment banking, markets and payments |
| Asset & Wealth Management | Asset management, private banking and wealth management |
| Corporate | Corporate-level activities and adjustments |

### 📦 Product-Level Analysis

Revenue is further analyzed across major product and business categories to identify the strongest contributors to overall performance.

This allows users to understand **which products and services are driving revenue growth.**

### 📅 Quarterly Trend Analysis

The dashboard compares financial performance across reporting periods to identify:

- Revenue growth or decline
- Net income movements
- Segment changes
- Product-level movements
- Emerging performance trends
